How the transcript feature works

Not translation between languages — translation from raw conversation into something worth reading. Four steps, no manual write-up.

01 Paste the meeting transcript

Drop the raw call transcript or notes into a venture's stand-up page — no formatting or cleanup needed first.

02 Rimthan structures it

Priorities, wins, risks, blockers and leadership asks get pulled out and sorted into the same fields your team already reports on.

03 Review and fine-tune

Nothing publishes blind — a venture lead can edit any field before it's shared, the same way they'd mark up a doc.

04 Share the read-back

Anyone who missed the call — or wants to check back weeks later — opens the venture's page and reads the stand-up, not the transcript.
