How we build ventures

Seven phases from first client conversation to final handoff — every venture on this dashboard sits somewhere along this path.

01 Sales & engagement · ~4–8 weeks

First conversations with a client or partner — scoping what the opportunity actually is before anything is scheduled.

02 Opportunity area assessment · ~2–4 weeks

Sizing up where the real leverage is, so effort goes toward the venture worth building — not just the first idea raised.

03 Ideation · ~1 month

Generating concrete venture concepts against the opportunity, and screening out the ones that don't hold up.

04 Validation · ~9 weeks

Testing the concept against real users and early demand — the stage most of this week's ventures are sitting in right now.

05 Incubation · ~6–7 months

Building the MVP and proving the model works in practice, not just on paper.

06 Commercialization · ~9 months

Scaling go-to-market, sales, and revenue until the venture can stand on its own.

07 Handoff protocol · ~3 months

Transferring ownership and day-to-day operation to the client or founding team — this is when a venture gets marked Completed.
